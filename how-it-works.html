How Zabbadat works

Seven steps from your first rep to league night.

Start playing Read the rules
  1. Pick a game. Try Washers, TUT push-ups, or Plank.
  2. Sign in. Your name then stays the same on every device and on the boards.
  3. Use the camera counter. For push-ups, set the phone on the floor under your chest. The camera counts each full rep and times your tension. Video never uploads.
  4. Battle a friend. Make a room, share the code, and both tap ready. A shared countdown starts the match.
  5. Earn and wager ZAB. Logged work earns ZAB, and a battle can carry a wager. See the rates.
  6. Build your reputation. Clean finishes add points, forfeits cost them. See the tiers.
  7. Come to league night. Sign the list on the home page, then bring washers or use the house box. Read about Zabbadat, also searched as Zabadat.

Everything is an honor system, and none of it is medical treatment. The washers rules are in the official regulations.
