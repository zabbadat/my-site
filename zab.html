ZAB, the Zabbadat game currency

Earn ZAB for the work you log. Wager it in battles if you like.

See your ZAB balance Open the battles

ZAB is the currency of Zabbadat. You earn it by logging real work, and it is shown with a washer-ring icon. Your balance and recent earnings appear after you sign in.

How you earn ZAB

  • 10 push-up reps = 1 ZAB
  • 1 minute of plank = 1 ZAB
  • 1 minute of TUT (time under tension) = 1 ZAB
  • 10 minutes of walking = 1 ZAB
  • Winning a signed-in, verified Zabba Washers game to 21 = 1 ZAB

Each set or walk counts on its own, and leftovers do not carry over.

Wagering ZAB in battles

TUT Battles, Plank Battles, and the Zabba Washer Battle can carry a wager. The person who makes the room sets the amount, with a minimum of 1 ZAB. Both players put in the same amount, and the countdown cannot start until both have. The server holds the pot, which is twice the wager, and the winner takes it. A tie, a draw, or a match that never starts refunds both players. If a player leaves or goes silent for 30 seconds, the other player wins.

Spending only happens through these server-checked wagers. Learn how fair play is scored on the reputation page, or see the games: Washers, TUT push-ups, and Plank.
