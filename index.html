Breathwork & Meditation — Sitkari coolbox breathing technique

Sitkari through the inhale, box counts on all four sides. Cool the mouth & mind, then close the cycle through the nose.

How to sitkari

The inhale

Sit or stand tall. Soften the jaw. Bring the teeth to a light edge — not a clench. Tongue rests behind the teeth or at the ridge. Draw air in through the mouth with a thin hiss, like steam through a small gap. Four counts in. Do not gasp. Shoulders stay down.

The box

One cycle, same length on every side:

Inhale mouth (Sitkari) — 4
Hold full — 4
Exhale nose — 4
Hold empty — 4

That is ~16 seconds. Lips close on the hold and on the nose exhale. No force on the empty hold.

How long

Full set: 18 cycles = about 5 minutes. If 4-counts feel tight, use 3-3-3-3 and do 24 cycles. Same five minutes. Five minutes is the allotted cooling box so the effect holds.

Bariboshi: both sides, one mandatory minute before the opening washer. Four 4-count cycles (~1 minute), or five 3-count cycles. Then the pitch.

Stop if you get dizzy, tingly, or short of breath. This is a settling drill before a match, not medical treatment.

Quick version: mouth in 4, hold 4, nose out 4, hold empty 4. Full steps, timing, and the Bariboshi minute are in How to sitkari above.

TUTS & PUSH up sets

  • As many push-ups you can do without quitting, or holding yourself up while holding a plank push press, with good form and a controlled tempo — not too fast — within a minute and twenty seconds. Even holding yourself up in a push-press plank is a big deal — you can't fail just by holding.
  • Lean your phone up in front of you, camera facing you. It counts each full set.
  • A set ends only when your knees touch down. Holding the top of a push-up press — a push-press plank — never ends it.
  • Hold the top 30 seconds straight for the hold bonus, once per set.
  • Same login as washers. This isn't a form judge, and it isn't medical treatment.

    Zabba Washer Battle

    One on one, or two teams of two, phone to phone, Bariboshi style. No video: you pitch at your own box and report each pitch, your opponent confirms or disputes, and the first to 21 wins. Make an arena, share the code, everyone taps ready, and a shared countdown starts the match. Pick 1v1 or 2v2: in 2v2 the team score is the sum of both teammates' packs, and the first team to 21 wins. The opening pitch (closest to the cup goes first), then rounds of one pack of 3 washers each, scored Bank 5, Shoot 3, Box-bound 2, Out 0. Your phone counts your steps as an optional extra. Forfeit, leave, or go silent for 30 seconds and your opponent wins. Optional ZAB wager: set the amount when you make the room, both players put in the same amount, and the winner takes the pot. See the official regulations.
