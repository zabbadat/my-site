Breathwork & Meditation — Sitkari coolbox breathing technique

Sitkari through the inhale, box counts on all four sides. Cool the mouth & mind, then close the cycle through the nose.

Sitkari

Inhale through the mouth

Light contact of the teeth, tongue behind them or at the ridge, air drawn in with a soft hiss. This is the cooling inhale. Do not gasp. Four counts in.

Box

The four sides

Keep this cycle the entire time: inhale through the mouth — 4, hold — 4, exhale through the nose — 4, hold empty — 4. That is one cycle (~16 seconds).

5 minutes

18 cycles

Do 18 cycles to land on 5 minutes. If a 4-count feels tight, use 3-3-3-3 and do 24 cycles. Same five minutes. Five minutes is the allotted time for a full Sitkari cooling box so the effect holds.

Bariboshi

Ceremonial 1 min

Before the opening Bariboshi, both sides take one mandatory minute of Sitkari cooling box. Same cycle: mouth in 4, hold 4, nose out 4, hold empty 4. Four cycles (~1 minute). If using 3-counts, five cycles. Then the pitch.

Sit upright. Stop if you get dizzy. This is a settling drill before a Bariboshi, not a medical treatment.
