Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026. This page is public and does not require sign-in.

This policy explains how Zabbadat (zabbadat.com and the ZabbaDat app) handles a few kinds of information. We only describe practices in use today.

Camera video

When you use the camera for push-ups, planks, or similar tracking, the video is processed on your device to count reps and watch form. Camera video is never uploaded to Zabbadat or to our servers.

Google sign-in

Sign-in is optional. If you choose Sign in with Google, Google shares the account details needed to sign you in — typically your name, a unique account id, and your email — with Firebase Authentication. We use that so finished games can post under your name. You can stay signed out and still play; results then stay on your device and are not posted to the boards.

Names and scores stored in Firebase

If you are signed in and you finish a set or a game, your display name and scores are stored in Firebase. That includes things such as push-up counts, hold times, time-under-tension sets, and ZAB totals, so they can appear on public boards and related totals on the site. We do not post those results until you are signed in and a session finishes.

Local reminders

The ZabbaDat phone app can offer an optional daily reminder on your device. Those reminders are scheduled locally on the phone. They are not sent by our servers, and turning them off happens on the device.

Information that stays on your device

Some totals, such as today’s reps, sets, and time under tension, can also be saved on the device you are using, including when you play as a guest. Clearing the browser or app data on that device removes this local copy.

Site analytics

The website uses Google Analytics to measure visits to public pages, including this one. That is separate from camera video, which is not uploaded.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to zabbadat@mail.grokbot.com.
