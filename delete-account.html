Delete your Zabbadat account

For the Zabbadat website (www.zabbadat.com) and the ZabbaDat app for iPhone and Android. Updated November 10, 2026.

You can ask us to delete your Zabbadat account and the data tied to it at any time. You do not need to explain why.

How to ask

  1. In the app or on the website: open Zabbadat, sign in, and go to the sign-in area in the Play section of the home page. Tap Delete my account, then Email my deletion request. Your email app opens with the request and your account id filled in. Send it.
  2. By email: write to zabbadat@mail.grokbot.com with the subject “Delete my Zabbadat account”. Tell us the name you use on the boards and how you sign in (Google, X, or email), and send it from the email on that account if you can, so we can find it.

We may reply to confirm the request comes from the account owner before we delete anything.

What we delete

  • Your sign-in account (Google, X, or email sign-in) in Firebase Authentication, including the email, name, and photo it holds.
  • Your display name and board name, and your X handle if you added one.
  • Your scores and results: push-ups, planks, time-under-tension sets, washers games, and the boards they appear on.
  • Your step records: daily step totals, live walk sessions, and walk mirrors.
  • Your battle, reputation, and ZAB data: your ZAB balance and ledger, ZAB board entry, battle stats and season records, and reputation. ZAB has no cash value and cannot be restored once deleted.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Other players’ finished battles: records of a battle or wager you played with someone else are needed to keep their results and ZAB correct. We remove your name and account id from those records, so they are no longer tied to you.
  • Your deletion request email: we keep the email thread for up to 90 days after we confirm the deletion, as a record that we did it, and then delete it.
  • Form messages: messages you sent through a site form (such as a starter kit claim or order) are separate from your account. Ask in the same email and we delete those too.
  • Analytics: site analytics are kept according to Google Analytics retention settings and are not linked to your account.

Totals saved only on your own device are not on our servers. To remove them, clear the app or browser data, or uninstall the app. To stop step access on Android, turn off Zabbadat in Health Connect or your phone’s permission settings.

When it happens

We delete your account and data within 30 days of your request and reply by email when it is done. After that, signing in again with the same Google, X, or email account starts a new, empty account.
