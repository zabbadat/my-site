Plank House and Plank Battles

Longest good plank in the House wins. Last one holding wins the battle.

Start a plank hold Open Plank Battles

Plank House is the Zabbadat board for the longest plank. Prop your phone on the floor off to your side, a few steps away, with the screen facing you so the camera sees your whole body side-on, head to heels. The camera times your hold. It is an honor system, not a form judge and not medical treatment. Every 60 seconds of plank earns 1 ZAB.

Plank Battles

Challenge a friend to a plank, phone to phone. Make a room and share the 4-letter code. Both of you get the same countdown, then you hold from GO. When you drop, tap I dropped. The last one holding wins, and if you forfeit or leave, your opponent wins. Keep the page open, because switching away marks your hold unverified.

Wagers and reputation

A Plank Battle can carry an optional ZAB wager. The creator sets the amount, both players lock it in before the countdown, and the winner takes the pot. With a wager, the longest hold wins, holds within 1 second of each other are a draw and both wagers come back, and 30 seconds of silence counts as a forfeit. A clean finish adds to your reputation; a forfeit costs points.
