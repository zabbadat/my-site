About Zabbadat

Breathe. Activate. TUT. Score. Win. Rank. Reward.

Join league night Corporate wellness

Zabbadat (also searched as Zabadat) is a movement towards better health through activation, coordination, breathing, and meditation. It turns being active into a game: you walk, hold, push, and pitch, and the board keeps score.

The games

The Game of Washers, Bariboshi style, is the heart of it: one box, 20 feet, packs of 3, first to 21. Around it sit the TUT push-up board, Plank House, head-to-head battles, and ZAB, the currency you earn for the work you log. Reputation shows who plays fair.

League nights

The TZG DD League plays on league nights: one box, 20 feet, a pack of 3, first to 21 with the fewest steps and the fastest time. A house box is on site, and house rules are posted before the first pitch. Sign the list on the home page and we email the next date. Rules are in the official regulations.

Corporate wellness

For companies, Zabbadat is a live session and a walking contest, not another unused wellness app. A pilot is 45 to 60 minutes on site for up to about 20 people. A 4-week challenge, "21 and walk", and a seasonal plan are also available, along with custom Bariboshi boards. You get a board and a participation note.
